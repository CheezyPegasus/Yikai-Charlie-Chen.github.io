BIOGRAPHY

Hello! I’m Charlie, or Yikai Chen.

I’m currently studying Statistics and Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University, with plans to transfer into the School of Computer Science to pursue Artificial Intelligence. I have a strong interest in solving problems creatively—whether through code, data, design, or my own hands.

Whether I’m building data structures in C/C++, developing machine-learning projects in Python, or intonating and modifying instruments through luthiery, I aim to deliver clean, thoughtful results through streamlined workflows and careful execution.

Outside the classroom, you might find me playing music with @lingotheband, @chromiummusic, and @oneoneoneband; doing tricks and flips with @cmutricking; developing novel, delicious, and nutritious recipes in the kitchen; working on my own instruments inside CMU’s makerspaces; preparing for the next convention with @cmucosplay; or building my jewelry startup, @pittsburghpearls.
